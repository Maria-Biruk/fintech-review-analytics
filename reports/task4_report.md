Task 4: Insights and Recommendations
Objective: Synthesize the sentiment and thematic analysis into business-actionable insights, supported by clear visualizations and concrete product recommendations.

Instructions:

Insights
Identify at least 2 satisfaction drivers (e.g., fast navigation, reliable transfers) and 2 pain points (e.g., frequent crashes, failed OTP) per bank, with supporting evidence from the data.
Compare banks across key dimensions: overall sentiment distribution, average rating, and dominant themes.
Propose at least 2 concrete product or support improvements per bank, grounded in the data.
Visualization
Create 3–5 plots using Matplotlib or Seaborn:
Sentiment distribution by bank (e.g., stacked bar chart).
Rating distribution per bank (e.g., histograms or boxplots).
Top keywords or theme frequency per bank (e.g., horizontal bar charts or word clouds).
Sentiment trend over time (if date range is sufficient).
Git
Create a task-4 branch. Commit visualization scripts and the final report. Merge into main via a Pull Request.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

2+ drivers and 2+ pain points per bank, each supported by evidence from the analysis.
Clear, labeled, and well-titled visualizations.
Concrete, prioritized product recommendations per bank.

Minimum Essential To Do:

1 driver and 1 pain point per bank.
2 plots (e.g., sentiment bar chart and keyword frequency chart).
Final report of up to 10 pages in Medium blog style (PDF format).
