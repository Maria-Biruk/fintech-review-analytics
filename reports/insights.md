# Task 2: Sentiment & Thematic Analysis Report

## Overview

This analysis was conducted on Google Play Store reviews for three Ethiopian banking apps:

- Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)
- Bank of Abyssinia (BOA)
- Dashen Bank

A total of 2735 reviews were analyzed.

---

## 1. Sentiment Analysis Summary

- Positive reviews dominate overall (~58%)
- Negative feedback remains significant (~20%)

### Bank Comparison

- CBE: High positive sentiment but large volume of complaints
- BOA: Highest negative sentiment proportion → highest risk
- Dashen: Most stable sentiment balance

---

## 2. Key User Complaints

Common issues extracted from reviews include:

- App crashes / instability
- Transaction failures
- Slow performance
- Login and access issues
- Update-related bugs

---

## 3. Feature Requests

Users frequently express desire for:

- Faster transactions
- More stable mobile banking experience
- Improved login/authentication systems
- Better app updates and stability

---

## 4. Business Insights

### Retention Risk

BOA users show higher dissatisfaction → potential churn risk.

### Systemic Issue

Performance and transaction failures appear across all banks → indicates infrastructure or backend bottlenecks.

### Competitive Advantage

Dashen shows better user satisfaction → stronger UX reliability.

---

## 5. Recommendations

### For all banks:

- Improve transaction reliability
- Optimize app performance
- Fix login/authentication issues

### For BOA:

- Prioritize bug fixing and stability improvements immediately

### For CBE:

- Focus on scaling infrastructure due to large user base

### For Dashen:

- Maintain current system quality and enhance features
