# 📊 Fintech Review Analytics

A full end-to-end **data engineering + NLP analytics pipeline** that analyzes Google Play Store reviews from Ethiopian banking applications to extract customer sentiment, complaint themes, and business insights for product improvement.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

Mobile banking adoption in Ethiopia is rapidly increasing, but user experience issues such as transaction failures, login errors, and app instability continue to affect customer satisfaction.

This project transforms raw user-generated reviews into **structured business intelligence** using:

- Web scraping
- Data preprocessing
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Sentiment analysis
- TF-IDF thematic extraction
- PostgreSQL database engineering
- Data visualization
- Business insights generation

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Omega Consultancy aims to help Ethiopian banks improve digital banking services by analyzing customer feedback from Google Play Store reviews.

The goal is to:

- Identify customer satisfaction drivers
- Detect recurring complaints
- Improve product decision-making
- Support customer retention strategies

---

## 🏦 Target Institutions

- Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)
- Bank of Abyssinia (BOA)
- Dashen Bank

---

## 🧠 Key Problem Areas Identified

- Transaction failures
- Login & OTP issues
- App crashes
- Slow performance
- Update instability
